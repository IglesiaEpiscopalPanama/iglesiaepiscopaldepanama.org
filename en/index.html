About us

A shared faith.
A living history.

The Anglican presence in Panama dates back to the mid-nineteenth century. Today, that tradition continues in the life of our parishes and missions.

The Episcopal Church of Panama is a Christian religious organization in the Anglican tradition. Our identity is expressed through prayer, community life and pastoral care.

We are part of the Anglican Church of the Central American Region (IARCA), within the Anglican Communion.
